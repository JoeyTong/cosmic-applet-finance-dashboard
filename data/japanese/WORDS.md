# Bundled word lists

`words.json` is generated by `scripts/build_words.py`. The applet embeds it and
does not download the lists at runtime.

## What each library contains

| Library | JLPT band | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Elementary | N5 | 小学 |
| Junior high | N4 | 初中 |
| Senior high | N3 | 高中 |
| University | N2 and N1 | 大学本科 |

A word is placed in the easiest band that claims it. The four libraries do not
share a headword and reading. These are foreign-learner JLPT bands. They are
not the vocabulary lists of Japanese school textbooks.

## Sources

Level membership comes from [elzup/jlpt-word-list](https://github.com/elzup/jlpt-word-list)
(MIT), which is based on [jamsinclair/open-anki-jlpt-decks](https://github.com/jamsinclair/open-anki-jlpt-decks)
(MIT) and the community lists that trace back to tanos.co.uk.

Readings and short English glosses come from JMdict through
[jmdict-simplified](https://github.com/scriptin/jmdict-simplified) 3.6.2,
dictionary date 2026-11-16, when a headword matches. JMdict is
CC BY-SA 4.0, copyright the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group.
https://www.edrdg.org/jmdict/j_jmdict.html

A row with no JMdict match keeps the reading and gloss from the JLPT list.
`words.json` therefore contains CC BY-SA 4.0 dictionary text and stays under
CC BY-SA 4.0. The study sources copied into Finance Dashboard keep their
MPL-2.0 headers. This file is not relicensed.

Finance Dashboard shows the bundled headword and one gloss, then requests
fuller senses, examples, and pronunciation from Jisho and English Wiktionary.
Those responses are cached locally and are not stored in this file.
